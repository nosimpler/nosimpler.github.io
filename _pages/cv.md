## Research interests

Synchrony and symmetry in network dynamics (coupled-cell networks, graph fibrations, equitable partitions); neural oscillations and their role in perception; sleep neurophysiology; methods for large-scale neural data, from EEG to two-photon imaging and connectomics.

## Research positions

**Baylor College of Medicine**, Department of Neuroscience (remote), 2023–2026  
*Senior Scientific Programmer*  
Analysis, methods and research infrastructure for a large systems-neuroscience lab working on two-photon imaging, neuromodulation and connectomics.
- *Analysis and methods:* implemented causal and noncausal Wiener deconvolution to estimate neurotransmitter dynamics from fluorescence traces (Neyhart et al. 2024); contributed to the evaluation of the JEDI voltage sensors (Land et al. 2026); analyzed cortical acetylcholine dynamics and neural–astrocyte coupling during behavior.
- *Connectomics:* developed geometric graph neural networks (PyTorch Geometric) for automated proofreading of electron-microscopy volumes, including MICrONS and H01, and a pipeline for training and comparing models.
- *Infrastructure:* was a primary maintainer of the lab's shared two-photon imaging pipeline (Python, DataJoint), covering segmentation, motion correction, autoprocessing and job orchestration; built `datapal`, a dashboarding web application (FastAPI, Panel, Kubernetes).

**Brigham and Women's Hospital / Harvard Medical School**, Department of Psychiatry, 2020–2021  
*Research Scientist*
- Developed a deterministic group NMF procedure for phenotyping large sleep EEG datasets, a global phase-locking method for high-density EEG, and models of sleep onset

**Broad Institute of MIT and Harvard**, Stanley Center for Psychiatric Research, 2018–2020  
*Postdoctoral Research Associate*

**Brown University**, Department of Neuroscience, 2015–2018  
*Postdoctoral Research Associate*
