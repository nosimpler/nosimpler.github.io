In this I can make two commitments to you, my increasingly improbably human reader:

1. Posts here will be entirely human-written.

Why? Internet death spiral aside, my main rationale for having a blog is that *I like writing*. Exceptions to this rule will include most code --- including site design and other such nonsense: in fact if I ever write code by hand, I probably won't shut up about it. 

As for LLM editorial suggestions, I'm still torn. I'll update as that changes.

2. Relevant chats will be linked separately.

This is for posterity. Anything correct or useful on this blog is likely to involve my own clumsy and perhaps outright stupid engagement with the history of human thought (sometimes via an LLM), before trying to make myself laugh writing about it (see Commitment 1). 

I may edit linked chats if they go wildly off-topic midstream, but I will try not to censor germane but wrong ideas of my own, or especially to hide important insights that should not be attributed to me. These will be markdown copies of chats, not links. Deanonymize whichever model or models I'm using if you like, but I don't intend to refer to any particular company inline.

Anything incorrect: well, as they say, *caveat lector*. But if you do notice something wrong in a post, please alert me and I'll do my best to fix it quickly.
