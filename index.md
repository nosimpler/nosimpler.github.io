I'm a computational biologist whose work spans dynamical systems theory, biophysical modeling, and large-scale neural data analysis. I received a PhD in Cognitive and Neural Systems from Boston University and a BSc in Physics (emph. astrophysics) from Harvey Mudd College. I've held research positions at Brown University, the Broad Institute, Harvard Medical School and Baylor College of Medicine, and I've consulted for Cognito Therapeutics and the Levin Lab at Tufts University. My publications cover how brain rhythms are generated and how they affect perception, as well as sleep neurophysiology, neuromodulator dynamics, and bioelectric memory in nonneural cells.
