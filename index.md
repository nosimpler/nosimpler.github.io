I'm a computational neuroscientist and mathematical biologist who works at the intersection of dynamical systems theory, biophysical modeling, and methods/software for large-scale neural data analysis. 

I've held research positions at Brown University, the Broad Institute, Harvard Medical School and Baylor College of Medicine, and I've consulted for Cognito Therapeutics and the Levin Lab at Tufts University. I received a PhD in Cognitive and Neural Systems from Boston University and a BS in Physics from Harvey Mudd College. 

My published work includes articles on how brain rhythms are generated and how they affect perception, on sleep neurophysiology in schizophrenia, on neuromodulator dynamics during oculomotor behavior, and on bioelectric memory in nonneural cells.
