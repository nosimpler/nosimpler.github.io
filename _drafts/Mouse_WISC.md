### GEVI

In order to see what forebrains do while playing this adorable little mouse game, the authors bred their mice to express genetically encoded voltage indicators or GEVIs: proteins that --- when hooked up to a battery (by some wonder of quantum chemistry) --- become fluorescent. 

Just how fluorescent these proteins get depends on the voltage across the terminals of that battery. Cell membranes being the original [link] batteries, any measured fluorescence is really telling us about the membrane potentials of cells expressing the GEVI.
