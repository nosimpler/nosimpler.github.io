Coequivalences

### Grossberg's Turkey-Lover Fiasco

Not all identifications are allowed, of course; nor should they be. That is to say, our brains must constrain --- and otherwise structure --- which identifications are admissible. 

To restate Grossberg's famous example: If we conflate "eat" and "make love to", then how do we not make love to a Thanksgiving turkey? 

Such structure can be context-dependent. we do not wish to declare our lover and a turkey equivalent in general, but in particular contexts they are equivalent --- for instance, under the equivalence "at thanksgiving dinner".
